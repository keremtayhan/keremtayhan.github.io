STATEMENT

I make games, music, interfaces, and other digital things that are supposed to feel alive. I’m interested in atmosphere, system design, visual identity, and work that has an actual point of view.

A lot of what I do lives between engineering and art direction. I like building interactive experiences with strong mood, deliberate style, and a sense that someone real made them.

This site is an archive of that process: projects, experiments, fragments, logs, and things still becoming themselves.
